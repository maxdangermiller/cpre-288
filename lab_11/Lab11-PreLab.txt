Questions: 

1.  How does the servo motor work? 
		For example, what are the pulse widths necessary for full clockwise, center, 
		and full counter-clockwise positions? (1pt)

The servo uses pulse width modulation (PWM), with these pulses being between 750 to 2250 μs long. 

0 deg: 		750μs 	= 750000ns
90 deg: 	1500μs 	= 1500000ns
180 deg: 	2250μs	= 2250000ns


2.  What Timer1 registers are relevant to this lab? (2pts)

Timer 1B Registers since the servo is connected to PB5

- GPTMTBILR: 	Timer B Interval Load 	(16 bit)
- GPTMTBPR:		Timer B Prescale 		(8 bit)

- GPTMTBMATCHR:	Timer B Match 			(16 bit)
- GPTMTBPMR:	Timer B Prescale Match 	(8 bit)

- GPTMTBMR:		Timer B Mode
- GPTMCTL:		GPTM Control
