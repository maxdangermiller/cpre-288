2)  Given the difference in timer counts (int delta) between the edges of a SONAR pulse 
    (from sending a sound to receiving it back), what would you multiply delta by in order to get a 
    distance measurement in units of cm? (Hint: The speed of sound is 34,000 cm/sec) [1 points]

16MHz = 6.25 × 10⁻⁸ seconds

34,000 cm/sec

1/16MHz

(delta * 1/16MHz * 34000cm/sec) / 2 = dist (cm)
